# Original universe baseline

Working title: **Aerial Vanguard: Relay**

## The fantasy

Players become **Relay**, a high-mobility urban responder who redirects momentum through a pair of field-engineering gauntlets called the **Vector Loom**. The gauntlets project short-lived electromagnetic filaments into conductive architecture and municipal relay nodes. Relay can tether, arc between towers, redirect a fall, pull objects, and transfer stored motion into rescue or combat maneuvers.

The filament is engineered energy—not biology, silk, a web, or an animal-themed power. Traversal should feel like piloting a human-scale kinetic system: choose a line, load tension, trade altitude for speed, release cleanly, and improvise with the city.

## Hero silhouette

- Callsign: Relay
- Role: independent disaster-response vanguard and infrastructure investigator
- Suit language: graphite protective weave, asymmetrical cyan field conduits, amber load indicators, reinforced forearms and boots
- Key silhouette: twin forearm field housings and a narrow back-mounted energy spine
- Movement attitude: precise, athletic, rescue-first, with visible momentum management
- Explicit exclusions: insect/spider imagery, red-and-blue web patterns, eye shapes associated with protected superhero suits, copied emblems, or familiar character poses

The civilian identity, supporting cast, and final origin are intentionally unlocked until narrative work has its own originality review.

## Veyra City

**Veyra** is a fictional coastal vertical metropolis rebuilt around storm-energy collectors, sky transit, floodable lower streets, maintenance bridges, and a public network of relay pylons. Its traversal grammar comes from infrastructure rather than a real city map:

- dense mid-rise canyons for tether rhythm;
- tall collector spires for dives and aerial tricks;
- broad civic roofs for parkour and combat;
- elevated transit and maintenance lines for precision routes;
- storm barriers and old flood districts for layered exploration;
- interiors only where missions justify their memory and art cost.

No real-world or copyrighted game city map is imported. Districts will be generated from original block plans and then art-directed.

## Tone and themes

- competence under pressure;
- public infrastructure as a shared responsibility;
- movement as problem-solving;
- optimism earned through repair and rescue;
- consequences without graphic presentation.

## Originality checks

Before accepting a character, suit, gadget, faction, mission, district, logo, animation, or line of dialogue:

1. Describe its purpose without naming another franchise.
2. Identify at least three project-specific design constraints that shaped it.
3. Compare silhouette, color, terminology, mechanics, and narrative function against obvious references.
4. Replace anything whose appeal depends on recognition of someone else's protected expression.
5. Record external asset provenance in `ASSETS.md`.

Studying the broad principles of readable traversal, camera comfort, animation blending, open-world pacing, and responsive combat is allowed. Reproducing protected expression is not.
