# Optional paid assets

Last reviewed: 2026-08-18

No paid asset is approved or required for Milestones 0 or 1. Nothing has been purchased.

When a paid asset is genuinely useful, add it here before any purchase decision with:

- exact product and creator;
- stable marketplace URL;
- current price and sale status;
- complete licence and seat requirements;
- expected use and measurable time saved;
- mobile suitability evidence (triangle counts, textures, shaders, LODs);
- cheaper/free alternatives;
- integration and modification estimate.

Paid assets remain optional until the user explicitly approves a purchase. They must never be treated as project dependencies merely because they appear in this list.
